# Corrections and resource suggestions

This documentation is maintained by Remove Background From Video. Useful corrections help readers choose a workflow and verify a result.

For a feature, price or compatibility correction, include:

- The affected passage and the proposed change.
- An official documentation URL, or a reproducible test with permission to publish its files.
- The review date, app/model version, operating system and relevant settings.
- Whether the evidence is a documentation claim or an actual test.

For an external resource, describe the question it answers and any relationship between its publisher and the submitter. Suggestions are reviewed for practical value and accuracy. If an entry is sponsored in the future, identify that relationship beside the link.

Keep customer files, account details and secrets out of issues and pull requests. Billing or account support belongs with the [website operator](https://removebackgroundfromvideo.org/about).

All benchmark results must be real and clearly distinguish generated source footage from camera/customer footage. Do not submit invented rankings, endorsements, usage counts or software screenshots.
